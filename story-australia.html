From Australia back home again

I started 2024 on the other side of the world. My friend Jennifer – one of my international partners, the founder of The Create Escape – welcomed me in Sydney. We saw the Opera House, swam over the reefs off Byron Bay and walked through the rainforest.

Back to Italy, as a host

Then I came home to welcome travellers in Tuscany and Le Marche. The 2024 season brought together many of the people I love working with:

  • Learning Together in a Special Place – the yearly retreats for educators in Mercatello sul Metauro, with sessions on professional development and educational leadership.
  • Making Music in Italy – a singing programme with three maestros and local musicians, between Tuscany, Florence, Urbino and the hills of Le Marche.
  • The Creative Mind, Body and Spirit – ten days of art and wellness led by Sue Primeau and Nancy Olker, in Le Marche and on the island of Ischia.

Embracing Italian living

After every journey I am happy to slow down again to the Italian pace: discovering hidden gems, going to local festivals and meeting the people who live here. Follow me to see where I go next.

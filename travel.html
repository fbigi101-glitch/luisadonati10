My favourite Italy

Three places that grew up with me

[Urbino in the autumn fog]

Le Marche · Mercatello & Urbino

The region on the border with Tuscany, where I spent many teenage summers among colourful woods, swimming rivers and medieval villages. Local artists and artisans, cooking classes, pottery and printing workshops.

When
Late April to November. June–mid September for a rural summer; October–November for truffles, mushrooms and Urbino in the fog.
How long
At least 7 days, to slow down to local rhythms.

Tuscany · Florence & Siena

Florence is my city. We walk the areas locals love, visit my leather and ceramics artisans and a young Italian fashion designer, then stay in the countryside to explore Siena, Lucca, Pienza, Cortona, Chianti and the Val d'Orcia.

When
Late April to early October for the countryside; the art cities are lovely all year – beware the summer heat.
How long
7 days for the Tuscan way of life; 3 days for one art city.
[An Oltrarno workshop, Florence]
[La Corricella, Procida]

The Gulf of Naples · Ischia & Procida

Procida, the smaller (not younger) sister; Ischia with its volcano walk among ancient woods, the Lacco Ameno shoemaker who makes sandals on the spot, and thermal baths by the sea.

When
Late April–May, when the air smells of zagare (lemon blossom); September–October too.
How long
At least 7 days, with one day in Naples.

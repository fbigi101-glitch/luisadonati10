Reviving a hidden gem in Chianti

In February 2023 I took on a project I loved from the very first visit: relaunching Podere Lupinati, an old agriturismo in the Chianti hills that had been closed and abandoned for a couple of years. The goal was to bring the hamlet back to life in time for the 2023 season.

A hamlet with a story

Podere Lupinati is made of a watchtower, the old farmer’s house and its annexes, restored in the early 1980s, with five units for guests.

From research to the first guests

I started with research: market trends, demand, prices and the right way to promote the property. Then I went through every building and listed what it needed to open – new furniture, improvements to bathrooms and kitchens, the swimming pool and the garden furniture.

With a wonderful team we made it in time. Welcoming the first guests and reading their enthusiastic feedback was pure joy, and the project reminded me how much I love this work.

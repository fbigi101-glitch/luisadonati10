My story

I've literally spent my life travelling.

Born in Rome into an old Italian family, raised between Rome, Tuscany and Le Marche, then London – and now Florence.

In London I worked on exhibitions, concerts and festivals – always trying to give both visitors and performers a wonderful experience.

Hospitality came to me through my family. With my brothers and sisters I turned our Tuscan farm into a holiday resort, restoring villa after villa in an almost abandoned hamlet – and later did the same in Le Marche.

Today I live in Florence and spend as much time as I can in Mercatello sul Metauro, where I now welcome retreats and courses at Abbondanza and its garden.

I love jazz and classical music, and I love people. Everywhere I go, I find friends – and that's the Italy I want to share with you.

— Luisa
